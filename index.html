Ethical & Privacy Principles

  • No Demographic Bias: Sensitive personal attributes (e.g., race, gender, socio-economic status, ZIP code) are strictly excluded to avoid historical algorithmic stereotyping.
  • Local Computation: All CSV parsing, data transformations, and predictions occur entirely inside your web browser. No student records are transmitted to third-party cloud servers.
  • Formative Early Warning: Outputs are explicitly designed as guidance for academic advisors to offer help, not punitive or summative evaluation.
